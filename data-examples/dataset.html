Beginner 5K Weekly Training Plan

Goal: Build consistency, aerobic base, and confidence over one week.

Day Workout Duration Notes
Monday Easy walk-run intervals 30 minutes Alternate 2 minutes jogging with 2 minutes walking.
Tuesday Mobility and light strength 25 minutes Focus on hips, calves, glutes, and core.
Wednesday Easy continuous jog 20 minutes Keep the pace conversational.
Thursday Rest Full day Prioritize hydration and sleep.
Friday Walk-run intervals 35 minutes Alternate 3 minutes jogging with 2 minutes walking.
Saturday Long easy walk or jog 40 minutes Use an easy effort and avoid speed work.
Sunday Recovery walk 20 minutes Keep it relaxed and gentle.
